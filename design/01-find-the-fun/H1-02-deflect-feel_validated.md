# H1-02 · Deflect feels fair

- **IF/THEN:** IF the deflect window is generous (≥0.4 s) and incoming bullets telegraph clearly, THEN the owner lands at least 7 of 10 intended deflects in a greybox wave.
- **Source section:** §3 Core Loop — DEFLECT step
- **Cheapest killing test:** same desktop Explorer greybox as H1-01, owner self-test counting 10 deliberate deflect attempts
- **Key metric:** deflect success rate on intended attempts; below 70% = failed
- **Mobile-sensitive:** yes
- **Tested on:** desktop (criterion measured) · mobile (owner self-test — kill-check held, criterion not measured)
- **Parked:** 2026-08-13

## Brief

- **Criterion (external):** deflect success rate on intended attempts in one greybox wave — at least 7 of 10 (≥70%), with at least 10 intended attempts made; below 70% = failed. An intended attempt is an E press while the deflect is off cooldown (presses during recharge do nothing and are not counted — established H1-01 pass 1→2); a success is a press while a bullet is telegraphing blue. Failure looks like: the owner deliberately hunts deflects and still whiffs more than 3 presses in 10 — the window reads as luck, not timing.
- **Kill-check (owner-testable):** before pressing E, the owner can predict whether the deflect will land — a landed deflect feels earned by timing, not granted by chance. If deflects feel like coin flips even when they succeed, the claim is dead.
- **Verdict ceiling (pre-registered):** unlike H1-01, the criterion here is *mechanically counted* (HUD attempt/success counters, agent-verified in the H1-01 smoke) and the claim's subject is the owner themselves — so a measured ratio ≥7/10 may close **`validated`** (desktop; renders *mobile pending* since `Mobile-sensitive: yes`). If the ratio holds but the kill-check fails (numbers pass, feel says lucky), that conflict is raised, never blended into one verdict.
- **Note on the verb:** this tests the **aimed** deflect — camera-direction ricochet, kept by owner decision 2026-08-13 (`decisions.md`) — not the auto-return version H1-01's passes 1–4 played. Prior ratios (40/43 auto-return, 13/17 mid-look-see) are context, not evidence.
- **Rung:** desktop Explorer — the window/telegraph feel cannot be settled by arithmetic; mobile (touch) is a separate open question, deliberately left as *mobile pending*.
- **Who tests:** both — agent MCP smoke first (scene running, counters zeroed, no errors), then the owner by hand; only the owner's pass counts.
- **Who launches:** agent — preview server already running; agent launches desktop Explorer via deep link (owner requested).
- **Real:** the full deflect chain as decided: single lane-threat telegraph (nearest approaching bullet whose path passes within 1.2 m, acquired at 3.5 m, sticky to 4.3 m, ~0.9 s of blue ≈ 0.7 s to impact — satisfies the IF's ≥0.4 s window), 1.5 s cooldown, camera-aimed outgoing direction at 6 m/s.
- **Faked:** everything H1-01 faked — greybox arena, no art, no other players, no progression/score/reward.
- **Instrumented:** existing HUD counters (attempts/successes, smoke-verified), `SMOKE deflect attempt` log lines, click-to-copy panel. Counters zeroed by the fresh Explorer launch.
- **Not building:** nothing — the build exists. Explicitly out of scope: tuning window, cooldown, lane width or bullet speed mid-pass (any tuning is an instrument change, logged between passes); new mechanics; scoring.
- **Sessions:** owner alone; one wave per pass (~40 s), one pass may settle it; up to 2–3 passes with logged instrument fixes.
- **Task given to the tester:** "Play one full wave and deliberately try to deflect — at least 10 attempts. When a bullet flashes blue, press E; aim your camera to choose where it flies. When the wave ends, click the HUD panel and paste the line."
- **Collected per session:** deflect attempts and successes (the metric) · whether you could tell before pressing if it would land · times hit, min FPS (riders) · anything said unprompted.
- **Briefed:** 2026-08-13

## Mobile rung — brief (2026-08-19)

Re-test on device, appended per the contract (same ID, same file; `Tested on` gains `mobile` only at a verdict).

- **Criterion (external, unchanged from the desktop brief):** deflect success rate on intended attempts in one greybox wave, **on the owner's phone** — at least 7 of 10 (≥70%), with at least 10 intended attempts made; below 70% = the aimed deflect fails on touch. With three input paths now live, *intended attempt* is defined per path: an **E or F press** while off cooldown (presses during recharge do nothing and are not counted — H1-01 rule), or a **tap that lands on a spiral bullet** while off cooldown. A *success* is an attempt that connects with the telegraphing blue bullet. *Aim performed* means the outgoing direction is camera-forward on every path — tap deflects also fly where the camera points, so tapping does not bypass the aim part of the verb.
- **Counting honesty (pre-registered):** a tap that hits no bullet at all is *uncounted* — on touch it is indistinguishable from camera input, so the instrument cannot see it. This biases the tap path toward success; the criterion can still fail via taps landing on non-blue (magenta) bullets and via whiffed E/F button presses, both of which count as misses and spend the cooldown.
- **Kill-check (owner-testable):** on the phone, the owner can still predict before acting whether the deflect will land — touch makes it harder, not a coin flip. If deflects on device feel like luck even when they succeed, the mobile claim is dead.
- **Verdict ceiling:** HUD-counted ratio, same agent-verified instrument → a measured ≥7/10 may extend the `validated` verdict to `tested on: desktop + mobile` (clearing *mobile pending*); below 70% on device appends a mobile `failed` finding to this file and §3 must say the touch verb is open.
- **Rung:** mobile via Creator Hub QR preview — the question is the device.
- **Who tests / who launches:** owner self-tests on their phone (QR); agent runs the preview server and flips the density knob between phases.
- **Instrument/input changes from the desktop build (logged, verb untouched):** (1) **HUD fix** — root cause was not the device inset: on SDK 7.26+ a 16:9 virtual canvas is overridden to **1600×720 on mobile**, so the counters panel at `left: 1400, width: 460` overflowed the canvas by 260 px; fix is right-anchoring (`right: 20`), correct on both platforms, default `screenInset: 'device'` kept. (2) **F (`IA_SECONDARY`) as alternate deflect press**, same code path as E. (3) **Tap-the-bullet**: pooled bullets get `CL_POINTER` sphere colliders + a pointer-down handler; a tap on a spiral bullet off cooldown = one attempt (cooldown spent), success iff it is the blue target, outgoing direction camera-forward; walls never answer (H2-03 law); no hover feedback (greybox telegraph language stays: blue = press now). Desktop gains click-a-bullet as a side effect — harmless, desktop verdict already closed. (4) HUD copy line gains `platform=` so the pasted evidence names the rung. Window, cooldown, telegraph, lane rule: **frozen as validated**.
- **Not building:** desktop verb tuning; `TouchScreenControls` customization; new mechanics beyond the two input variants.
- **H1-03 rider (FPS at density, on device):** after the deflect passes, agent flips `DENSITY_TEST = 150` (hot-reload pushes it to the phone); owner reads the HUD `fps (min …)` line at full density against the program's **30 fps** minimum-hardware bar, plus a glance test (game or slideshow). **pass 2: owner QR self-test · 2026-08-19 · in flight, no counter line yet.** First on-device pass with the three input changes live. Owner mid-pass report: **the deflect almost never lands** and the run is "unplayable" because three hits knock them out before enough attempts accumulate. **pass 2 continued · 2026-08-19 · two findings, one amendment.** (1) **Tap-the-bullet does not fire on the mobile client** (the same build's tap works on desktop) — suspected client-side issue with entity pointer events from direct touch, not scene code; the tap path stays in the build for the record, E/F on-screen buttons are the working mobile inputs. (2) Even with knockout off, **camera-aim + press remains "super difficult" on touch** (owner). Instrument change between passes, at owner request: **mobile-only aim assist** — `isMobile()`-gated; when the camera points within a 60° half-angle cone of the yokai core at the moment a deflect connects, the outgoing shot snaps to the core; outside the cone it stays camera-forward; desktop path byte-identical to the validated verb. **Pre-registration amendment (recorded here, not silently edited into the brief):** on the mobile rung, *aim performed* now means "camera roughly toward the yokai (within 60°) when deflecting" — precision is assisted. The counted metric is untouched (success = connecting with the blue bullet; the assist only shapes the outgoing direction). Rider note: owner reports FPS "seems OK" at normal gameplay density — informal, not the H1-03 read; `DENSITY_TEST = 150` still pending.

**pass 3 prep · 2026-08-19.** Owner on the assist build: "it's better." Remaining readability gap: the cooldown state was only visible as the greyed corner-panel line — not glanceable while dodging on a phone. Instrument change between passes (owner request): **bottom-center recharge bar** — grey and filling while `deflectCooldown > 0` (focus on dodging), solid blue + "DEFLECT READY" when live, matching the blue telegraph language (bar blue = a bullet can flash blue). Feedback/readability only; window, cooldown and telegraph values untouched.

**pass 3 finding · 2026-08-19 · owner: "a lot better", but blue bullets at wave start sometimes ignored the deflect.** Code audit found two real defects, fixed as instrument hardening (no verb change): (1) **stale blue material on pooled reuse** — `despawnBullet` hid entities without repainting, so a wave's last blue bullet idled in the pool still-blue; at the next wave start the first spawns reuse those entities, and a client applying the visibility flip before the repaint (the mobile client already drops tap events, so its update path is suspect) shows a blue-looking bullet that is not the target and answers no input — matches "first bullets of the wave" exactly, and mobile's lower tick rate widens the window vs desktop. Fix: strip the telegraph material at despawn. (2) **ghost target** — a bullet recorded as the nearest deflect candidate could despawn later in the same loop iteration (by hitting the player) and still be assigned as `currentTarget`, letting the next press count a false success on an invisible bullet; fix: only an active `nearest` is assignable, and `attemptDeflect` requires an active target. (2) protects the metric's integrity; both fixes are desktop-inert in normal play. Known lookalike, by design: a bullet deflected point-blank at the core absorbs instantly (bar ticks, no visible flight).

**pass 3→4 tuning · 2026-08-19 (owner request):** mobile aim-assist cone halved, 60° → **30°** half-angle — the camera must point twice as precisely at the yokai for the snap; outside the cone the shot stays camera-forward. The session amendment's *aim performed* reads accordingly (camera within 30°). Criterion unchanged.

**pass 3→4 tuning, second change · 2026-08-19 (owner request):** on **mobile only**, a whiff press (E/F with nothing blue) no longer spends the cooldown — only a landed deflect does. Conflict raised and accepted: this relaxes the H1-01 anti-spam rule (a whiff costing the full 1.5 s is part of the desktop-validated verb — E-spam killed the weave in H1-01 pass 1) on the touch rung; desktop path unchanged. Metric integrity preserved: whiff presses still count as attempts on both platforms, so spraying still drags the ratio down. If the mobile verb ships with free whiffs, §3's verb description needs a per-platform note — flagged for the verdict write-back.
