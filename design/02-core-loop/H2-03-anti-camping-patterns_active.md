# H2-03 · Sweeping patterns refuse camping

- **IF/THEN:** IF the pattern law is redesigned so gaps sweep AND threat reaches every radius, THEN a deliberate camping round — staying inside one 2.5 m spot, rim included, micro-adjustments allowed, under the 3-hit death rule — ends in a knockout before the yokai is banished.
- **Source section:** §3 Core Loop — WEAVE ("the gap never opens in the same place") and the goal structure's Known break; graduated anti-camping idea (design law, not a bolted-on hazard)
- **Cheapest killing test:** the same H2 greybox with the new pattern law; the owner repeats H2-01's pass-2 rim-camp probe, judged by the existing camping meter (>50% camp fraction = a camp)
- **Key metric:** a majority-camped round that still banishes = failed (the pattern design is not doing the anti-camping work)
- **Mobile-sensitive:** no
- **Tested on:** —
- **Parked:** 2026-08-14 · sharpened 2026-08-15 against H2-01's failure data: the original "standing near-still gets hit" was passable at the rim with micro-movement while the design still failed — H2-01's spot-agnostic phrasing is the real kill condition. Now **load-bearing**: blocks §3's goal structure and §4.3 hook 1's "unfarmable by camping". H2-01's mechanical residue for the brief: deflect damage is position-independent while threat density thins with radius — sweeping safe zones alone does not answer a rim camp; pattern tunables frozen in H2-01 (17° spiral step, ±7° drift, 4 arms) may move; the 3-hit death rule interaction must be re-examined.

## Brief

- **Criterion (external):** camping meter + event log over **deliberate camping probe rounds** (owner playing adversarially: pick one 2.5 m spot — any spot: rim, mid, center, pit edge — micro-adjustments and jumps allowed, 3-hit rule live, deflects aimed at the emitter). Threshold: **zero majority-camped rounds (>50% camp fraction) reach a banish — every probe round ends in a knockout first.** Probes required: (a) the H2-01 pass-2 rim spot, repeated; (b) at least one more spot of the owner's choosing — the nastiest they can find. Failure looks like: any spot from which the bar melts to a banish while the meter reads majority-camp. Unlike H2-01's criterion this one is measurable *now*: the meter and the knockout/banish events are machine-logged; the owner supplies the adversarial play, not the judgement — so it can earn `validated`.
- **Kill-check (owner-testable):** after actively hunting for a farm spot, camping no longer reads as *the correct way to play* — the owner cannot name a spot they would stand in to win. If they find one the instrument missed, the law is dead regardless of what the meter says.
- **Rung:** desktop Explorer (Creator Hub default) — same H2 greybox, new pattern law; `Mobile-sensitive: no`. Arithmetic alone cannot settle it: the static-camper math is in this brief as design rationale, but the claim dies or lives on what an adversarial human finds (H2-01 proved exactly that — the sim camped the emitter, the owner found the rim).
- **The pattern law under test (what changes, and why each piece is pattern, not hazard):** H2-01's residue: deflect damage is position-independent while threat density thins with radius, and E answers any *single* threat every 1.5 s — so anti-camping must deliver, at **every** radius, threats that one E per cooldown cannot clear. Three changes, all made of the same readable bullets:
  1. **Wall volleys with sweeping gaps** — every ~8 s of wave time the yokai emits **two full-circle rings of tall pillar bullets ~1.0 s apart** (inside one deflect cooldown — that inter-ring delay *is* the anti-camping mechanism), expanding at 3 m/s, each ring with one ~45° gap; ring‑2's gap is offset ~100° from ring‑1's, and the gap azimuth sweeps ~80° per volley (direction flips per wave, random start per wave — "the gap never opens in the same place"). A static player deflects one wall's dead-on pillar (the deflect hole still works, for everyone — the H1 promise survives) but E is recharging when the second wall lands: ~1 guaranteed hit per wall-locked volley, ~4 volleys/wave → knockout inside a wave. A moving player tracks the sweep, reaches the gap, or punches through; the 3-hit rule is the forgiveness budget for the volley they misread.
  2. **Pillars, not spheres, for walls** — wall bullets are vertically stretched (~3.3 m top) so they cannot be jumped, and rings are 60 pillars so no standing seam exists between adjacent rays at any reachable radius (spacing ≤ 1.26 m < 2× hit distance); each ring gets a random azimuthal phase. Jumping and seam-riding were the two micro-adjustment outs the metric explicitly allows.
  3. **Bullets bloom from the core (spawn radius 1.2 → 0.3) and threaten the whole floor (hit reach to the floor edge, not the pit radius)** — closes the two geometric sanctuaries: standing on/next to the emitter (spiral density at r<3 saturates one E per 1.5 s by arithmetic) and straddling the soft pit edge (the fringe floor is hit-eligible; only the raised walkway — unreachable mid-wave without a knockout — is safe, which is its design role). Deflect, telegraph and the camping meter stay pit-only.
- **Death-rule interaction (re-examined, pre-registered):** the 3-hit knockout **stays** for this test. It is load-bearing in both directions: it forgives a mover's occasionally unreachable gap (worst-case cross-arena run can exceed the wall's travel time), while volley cadence (~4/wave, ~75% wall-locked for a static spot) still delivers ≥3 hits inside one wave to a camper. If probes land marginal (camper repeatedly escapes at 2 hits/wave), the registered lever is **volley cadence/inter-ring delay** — pattern tunables, unfrozen — never the criterion and never a silent death-rule change (that would be an owner decision, logged in `decisions.md`).
- **Who tests:** both — agent smoke first (no Explorer MCP in this session; headless logic harness like H2-01's, scripted rim camper / center camper / edge straddler / gap-following mover, confirming both verdicts are reachable), then the owner by hand: one free-play round (guard: the law must not visibly murder normal play; also H2-02 rider material), then the camping probes that decide the criterion.
- **Who launches:** the split H1-01/H2-01 recorded — agent runs the preview server for the smoke pass; owner launches via Creator Hub for their sessions.
- **Real:** the entire H1 verb, frozen — 1.5 s deflect cooldown, honest one-bullet telegraph, 1.2 m lane rule, aimed deflect, 4 m/s spiral bullet speed. The full H2-01 round structure, unchanged: life bar (18 HP), deflect-only damage, 3-hit knockout, wipe reset, ramp, clean streak. New and real: the pattern law above. Pattern tunables frozen in H2-01 (17° step, ±7° drift, 4 arms, emit tick, spawn radius) are **unfrozen** — any change logged per pass in `## Sessions`.
- **Faked:** unchanged from H2-01 — emitter sphere + primitive bar for the yokai, density numbers for the roster, solo (every knockout empties the pit), text banners for round transitions.
- **Instrumented:** everything H2-01 had (camping meter live + per-wave, HUD counters, click-to-copy line, SMOKE log lines) plus: **wall hits vs spiral hits split** per wave and per session, volley counter, and peak live-bullet count (60-pillar rings + deep-round spiral may brush the 150-projectile load H1-03 measured; minFps watches it).
- **Not building:** league board, Great Haunting, scrapbook UI, yokai art (owner's list); no third pattern element beyond spiral + walls, no audio, no menus, no persistence, no touch controls, no multiplayer, no arena geometry rework (no real sunken pit — the hit-reach rule stands in for it).
- **Sessions:** owner alone. Pass 1 free play (~5 min, one run). Passes 2+: camping probes, one round each (~2–4 min): rim repeat, then owner's-choice spots until the owner is satisfied no farm spot remains or one is found. Instrument fixes between passes logged here.

When you're done, click the panel and paste the line here." · camping probes — "Now try to break it: pick one spot — anywhere — and win from it without leaving. Small steps and jumps are allowed. Farm the bar from that spot until you're knocked out or the yokai is banished, then paste the line. Then try the nastiest other spot you can think of."

## Sessions

**smoke: agent manual dry-run (headless logic harness) · 2026-08-15 · passed.** No Explorer MCP in this session, so the smoke ran headless like H2-01's: the real `src/game.ts`/`state.ts` bundled against a mocked SDK (esbuild alias, harness in the session scratchpad) and driven at 30 ticks/s through four scripted players. Results, at the tunables now in the build: **rim camper** (H2-01's spot, r=11.5, optimal E aimed home) — knocked out in wave 1 of every run, 3 wall hits each, 98% camp fraction, bar never below 8/18, zero banishes across 5 seeded runs; **center camper** (r=0.8) — knocked out in ~2–8 s by spiral saturation from the new core spawn, zero banishes; **pit-edge straddler** (deflects at r=12.3, steps to r=13.0 when a wall closes) — knocked out wave 1 every run at 96–97% camp, confirming the floor-wide hit reach closed the soft-edge sanctuary; **skilled gap-mover** (hovers at the predictable next gap, sprints gap-1→gap-2, punches walls as fallback) — banished repeatedly, one run to round 5, wall hits ≈1 per 200 s, camp 4–30%. Both verdict directions are reachable with the instrument as built: the camper knockouts are the pass signature, and an earlier mistuned run (below) produced exactly the fail signature (banish at 97% camp) and the meter caught it. Peak simultaneous bullets 133–149 vs the 150 H1-03 measured — at the budget edge; `minFps` watches it in-world.

**Two pattern-law changes made during smoke (unfrozen tunables; criterion untouched):** (a) **wall telegraph shortened to 1.5 m / 0.5 s** (`WALL_TELEGRAPH_RANGE`; spirals keep 3.5 m, still exactly one honest blue) — at the spiral's 3.5 m telegraph a camper could press early on ring-1 and have E back before ring-2 landed, defeating the cooldown lock: the first smoke run banished round 1 at 97% camp. Lock condition recorded in the code: wall telegraph time < cooldown − inter-ring. (b) **ring-2 gap offset 100° → 45°** (= gap width) and **volley period 8 s → 7 s** — at 100° even the skilled mover ate a wall hit per volley (gap-2 unreachable in the 1.0 s window; the E-punch is a coin flip below ~r9 because ray spacing < 2× hit radius), so the law was killing movers too; at 45° the gap-1→gap-2 sprint is makeable and became the intended dance, while no standing spot satisfies both gaps beyond r≈3.2 (spiral saturation owns everything below r≈4). Geometric note for the owner's eye: a deflect hole in a wall is only passable outside r≈6.2 — closer in, the visible hole is narrower than the hit zones; if that reads as unfair in free play it needs a visual answer, not a tunable.

**Not covered headless** (first owner launch doubles as the visual check): pillar rendering (stretched spheres) and wall/gap readability at rim distance, whether the 0.5 s wall telegraph is human-pressable, real jump arcs vs the 3.3 m pillar top, in-world fps at peak bullets, hot-reload.

**in-world smoke, Explorer MCP · 2026-08-15 · passed.** The owner launched with `--mcp` and the tools bound; verified live: walls render and bloom (pillars visually merge at emissive 3 — readability flagged), volleys fire on the 7 s clock with sweeping gaps, wave transitions clean, an AFK avatar in the pit is knocked out and thrown to the walkway, peak 137 bullets at a steady 36–38 scene-tick fps, hot-reload works.

**pass 1: owner free play (two-ring law) · 2026-08-15 · desktop (Creator Hub/CLI preview, agent watching via Explorer MCP).** Owner's words, unprompted: *"now it's extremely difficult, but I think we are getting somewhere, the 2 walls approach can be good for super hard yokais."* Verdict on the two-ring volley as the baseline: too hard. Owner directed: **one wall per volley for this test**; floated two further ideas — distance-scaled deflect damage (bonus the closer you deflect to the yokai) and player-targeted bullets — both parked in `ideas.md` with the smoke analysis: aimed shots are dodged by a micro-sidestep inside the 2.5 m camp disc, so neither can carry the knockout criterion; they are pressure/reward levers, not the kill mechanism.

**law change between pass 1 and pass 2 (owner decision + smoke-forced consequence; criterion untouched).** One wall per volley — and because the first smoke proved a single *deflectable* wall is camp-food (punch the dead-on pillar, stand in the hole, every volley), **walls no longer telegraph and E does not answer them**: deep-red pillars, the only counterplay is the sweeping gap. "Press when blue" stays honest — walls simply never flash. Softened for movers at the same time: wall speed 3 → 2.5 m/s, gap 45° → 60°; two-ring tunables (`WALL_INTER_RING`, ring-2 offset, wall telegraph range) removed with the second ring; the two-ring volley is recorded in `decisions.md` as a hard-tier yokai signature. Re-smoke, all four scenarios: rim camper knockout wave 1 every run (98% camp, 3 wall hits, zero banishes), center camper knocked out in ~2 s, edge straddler knockout wave 1, gap-mover pays ~1 wall hit per 200 s (its losses are sim-grade spiral dodging, not walls), peak bullets 90–98. The law now discriminates: walls kill standing, movement makes them free.
