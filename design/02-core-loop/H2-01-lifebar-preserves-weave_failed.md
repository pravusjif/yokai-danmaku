# H2-01 · The life bar doesn't break the weave

- **IF/THEN:** IF the yokai has a visible life bar and deflected bullets are the only damage, THEN an owner self-test round still plays as weaving punctuated by deflects — the owner does not end up camping the 1.5 s cooldown near the emitter waiting for the next blue flash.
- **Source section:** §3 Core Loop — goal structure (decision 2026-08-14)
- **Cheapest killing test:** extend the H1 greybox with a life bar + deflect damage, death rule included (hit = out for the wave, streak resets — decision 2026-08-14); owner self-test, one full round
- **Key metric:** owner kill-check — if standing still and waiting out the cooldown reads as the correct way to play, the claim is dead. Riders: times hit vs the H1-01/H1-02 baseline, whether the one-wave knockout reads as sting rather than quit, whether the round still ends wanting another.
- **Mobile-sensitive:** no
- **Tested on:** desktop
- **Parked:** 2026-08-14

## Brief

- **Criterion (external):** camping fraction — % of a player's in-pit wave time spent inside a 2.5 m radius of one spot (movement instrument, per wave). Threshold: in v1 external playtests, **no more than 1 of 5 first-time testers plays a majority-camped round** (>50% camping fraction across the round's waves). Failure looks like: most testers discover the bar, plant themselves near the emitter, and win by waiting out the 1.5 s cooldown — the weave stops being the game. At v0 this stays unmeasured (needs external testers); it waits in §3 as `[HYPOTHESIS]`.
- **Kill-check (owner-testable):** one full round played to win (banish or wipe) still reads as weaving punctuated by deflects. If standing near the emitter and waiting out the cooldown reads to the owner as *the correct way to play* — the bar melts fine without moving — the claim is dead. Riders from the header: times hit vs the H1-01 baseline (5 hits over 3 waves, pass 4); does the one-wave knockout read as sting rather than quit; does the round end wanting another.
- **Rung:** desktop Explorer (Creator Hub default) — a feel claim about goal structure settles here; arithmetic cannot answer whether a life bar changes how the verb is played, and the claim is `Mobile-sensitive: no`.
- **Who tests:** both — agent smoke first (scene runs, bar damages, knockout/wipe/streak fire, counters record), then the owner by hand; only the owner's pass can judge the kill-check. Note: the Explorer MCP is not connected in this session — the smoke pass uses whatever transport is actually available and records which.
- **Who launches:** same split H1-01 recorded: agent runs the preview server for the smoke pass; owner launches via Creator Hub for their own sessions.
- **Real:** the entire H1 verb, untouched — 1.5 s deflect cooldown, honest one-bullet telegraph (blue = the one E sends back, only if its path would hit or graze), 1.2 m lane rule, aimed deflect (both cooldown and telegraph proved load-bearing in H1-01 and are frozen for this build). New and real: the yokai life bar, damaged **only** by deflected bullets that fly back into the emitter; the death rule (hit = out for the rest of the wave — thrown to the walkway, spectate, drop back in next wave; personal clean streak resets); the wipe reset (a wave ending with nobody standing in the pit resets the haunting to round 1); the ramp (banish → next round's pattern denser; waves tighten within a round; spiral direction and drift vary per wave so safe lanes sweep — H2-03's law, riding).
- **Faked:** the yokai is still the glowing emitter sphere with a primitive bar over it (no art); the roster/tiers are a density number; other players absent — solo, every knockout empties the pit, so every hit ends the run at wave end (§3 already names this collapse; it is the honest solo shape); "next round" preview is a text line. League board, Great Haunting, scrapbook, persistence: none.
- **Instrumented:** HUD counters — round + wave-in-round, yokai HP (world-space bar over the emitter + number), clean streak current/best, times hit, deflects, runs started, deepest round this sitting (rides for H2-02); **camping meter** — per-wave % of in-pit time spent within 2.5 m of where the player was 4 s earlier, shown live and logged per wave (`SMOKE` console line); click-to-copy line carries all counters.
- **Not building:** league board, Great Haunting, scrapbook UI, yokai art (owner's list, per invocation); also: no second authored pattern beyond the density/sweep ramp, no audio, no menus, no persistence, no touch controls, no multiplayer.
- **Sessions:** owner alone, self-testing; one pass = one full run (a few rounds to banish-or-wipe), ≈ 3–6 min; up to 2–3 passes with instrument fixes logged between them.
- **Task given to the tester:** "Drop into the pit and banish the yokai — empty its bar. Move with WASD; press E when a bullet flashes blue. If you get hit you're out until the next wave. Play until you've banished it or the run ends, then click the panel and paste the line here."
- **Collected per session:** camping fraction per wave (the instrument behind the kill-check) · times hit vs the H1-01 baseline · counter line at quit · whether the knockout read as sting or quit, and whether the round ended wanting another — owner's unprompted words first, direct question after.
- **Briefed:** 2026-08-14

**smoke: agent manual dry-run (headless logic harness) · 2026-08-14 · passed.** The Explorer MCP was not connected in this session (probe on 8123: connection refused) and binding it mid-session is a user action, so the smoke ran headless: the real `src/game.ts` + `src/arena.ts` + `src/state.ts` bundled against a mocked SDK surface (esbuild alias, harness in the session scratchpad) and driven at 30 ticks/s through three scripted scenarios. Verified mechanically: deflected bullets damage the bar **only** on reaching the yokai core (sim aimed at the emitter: 55/55 landed); bar empty → banish (round advances to 2, bar refills, next wave denser); hit → knockout (clean streak resets to 0, `movePlayerTo` throws the player to the nearest walkway point — (16, 3.2, 2) from a south hit); walking back into the pit while knocked out → re-teleported (0.6 s throttle); a wave ending with the player knocked out → wipe (back to round 1, runs++, "THE YOKAI WINS" banner); a clean wave → streak++ (best tracked); tail bullets in the rest gap sting without knockout; the camping meter reads **both directions** — 3% while circling, 8–29% under the sim's weave, 92–93% standing near the emitter — so both kill-check verdicts are reachable with the instrument as built. `npm run build` type-checks clean. **Not covered headless** (first owner launch doubles as the visual check): the world-space bar rendering/billboarding over the emitter, HUD layout, in-world `movePlayerTo`/knockback feel, hot-reload. Tuning observation, no change made: at perfect sim play the 18 HP bar empties in ~1.5 waves vs the design's ~3-wave target for a competently-deflecting *crowd* — a solo owner with real aim will be slower; recalibrate `BAR_HP` between passes if banish comes absurdly fast or slow, and log it here.

**instrument fixes between smoke and pass 1 · 2026-08-15.** (a) The world-space life bar rendered permanently black — the fill box sat inside the background box and was occluded from the camera side (exactly the rendering gap the headless smoke could not see); fill now protrudes through both faces of the billboard. (b) Death rule amended by owner decision on first launch, before play: knockout on the **3rd** hit per wave, not the 1st ("being out of the wave with 1 hit is too much"); any hit still resets the clean streak; solo wipe rule unchanged. Logged in `decisions.md` 2026-08-15. The criterion and kill-check did not move.

**pass 1: owner self-test, free play · 2026-08-15 · desktop (Creator Hub preview + agent watching via Explorer MCP).** Two reports mid-run: (a) *bug* — jumping over a bullet still counted as a hit; detection was horizontal-only (`HIT_DIST` ignored y). Fixed with a vertical overlap band (bullet clears when the player's feet top it); scene hot-reloaded, run state reset. (b) *unprompted, verbatim*: "nothing is really forcing me to avoid camping, I can easily find a place far away enough to just stand still and keep waiting for bullets to come my way and just hit E after the cooldown." The Brief's kill-check guessed the camp spot would be near the emitter; the owner found it at the rim — same claim (the header's metric line is spot-agnostic: "if standing still and waiting out the cooldown reads as the correct way to play, the claim is dead").

**pass 2: deliberate camping probe · 2026-08-15 · desktop.** Pre-registered verbally mid-session as the deciding probe — no threshold moved: play one full round deliberately camping the rim spot; does it carry to a banish and read as the correct way to play? Owner's pasted counter line: `H2 run=1 round=4 wave=1 deepest=4 streak=0/1 hp=18/18 deflects=55/61 yokaiHits=54 timesHit=6 camp=0% lastWaveCamp=70% wavesCompleted=6 bullets=32 fps=37 minFps=33`. Camping carried the run through **three banishes to round 4** in six waves: 55/61 deflects connected, 54 reached the emitter, 6 hits total, **zero knockouts**, 70% camp fraction on the last full wave. Owner's words: "I had to move a little" — micro-adjustment, not weaving. Mechanically why it wins: deflect damage is position-independent (an aimed green flies home from any radius) while threat density thins with distance from the emitter — at the rim the 4-arm spiral delivers roughly one threat per 1.5 s cooldown, which E answers on schedule. Note for the follow-up: the 3-hit amendment made the occasional camping hit nearly free (~1 hit/wave was absorbed without consequence); death rule and pattern law interact.

## Verdict
<!-- owned by /pre-prod-proto -->

**Verdict:** failed — kill-check did not hold: deliberate near-still camping carried the run to round 4 (3 banishes, 70% camp fraction, 0 knockouts); standing still and deflecting on cooldown plays as the correct way to play (owner self-test) · 2026-08-15 · tested on: desktop

What failed is scoped: the H1 verb itself is untouched and still proven (H1-01/H1-02) — what died is the claim that the life bar *as specified, under the current pattern law* (17° spiral step, ±7° drift, 4 arms) preserves the weave. The bar pays a stationary player at full rate. Constructive residue: anti-camping cannot ride as a 7° wobble — it must be a pattern-design law that actually forces movement, which is exactly parked H2-03; that hypothesis is now load-bearing and blocks the bar's return to §3 as settled truth. This probe's data is admissible as H2-03's starting evidence (a deliberate camp survived six consecutive waves) but H2-03 gets its own brief — its build may move the pattern tunables that were frozen here, and must re-examine the 3-hit death rule interaction.
